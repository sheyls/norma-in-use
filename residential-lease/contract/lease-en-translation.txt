STATE:
I.- That Ms Alice Doe is the owner of the dwelling located at [MUNICIPALITY], calle Ejemplo 1, 1ºA.
II.- That the owner (hereinafter, the landlord) expressly states that the dwelling described in the previous recital meets all the conditions and requirements necessary to be used to satisfy the permanent housing needs of the tenant party.
III.- That the landlord leases the said dwelling for its use by the tenant party as permanent residence, agreeing with her its occupation on that basis, and that, having reached agreement, they have decided to sign this RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT, which they do by means of this document and subject to the following clauses.

CLAUSES:
ONE. This agreement is made in accordance with Law 29/1994 of 24 November on Urban Leases, as adapted by Law 4/2013 of 5 June on measures to make the rental housing market more flexible and to promote it, and as amended by Royal Decree-Law 7/2019 of 1 March on urgent measures on housing and rental, and with what is agreed in this agreement.
The Civil Code applies on a supplementary basis.

TWO. The object of the lease is the dwelling located at [MUNICIPALITY], calle Ejemplo 1, 1ºA, together with the furniture and household goods detailed in the inventory which, signed by both parties, is attached to this agreement and forms an integral part of it, to be used exclusively as the tenant party's dwelling.

THREE. The rent agreed for this lease is ELEVEN THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED EUROS (11,400 euros) PER YEAR, that is, NINE HUNDRED AND FIFTY EUROS (950 euros) PER MONTH, which the tenant party shall pay within the first six days of each month. Once that period has elapsed without payment, the landlord may file a claim for termination of the agreement for non-payment, without any notice or demand.
Rent shall be paid in the landlord's name into the following account: [BANK], IBAN: [REDACTED]
During the first three years of this agreement, in the event that the agreement is extended, the said rent shall be updated annually in accordance with the legal reference index (IRAV) and on the terms established in article 18 of the Urban Leases Act in force.
After the first three years, if the agreement is extended, both its term and the rent shall be updated by agreement between the parties.

FOUR. The term of this agreement is ONE YEAR, beginning on 1 July 2026 (expiring on the first of July 2027). On expiry, the agreement shall be compulsorily extended for annual periods until the lease reaches a minimum duration of five years, unless the tenant notifies the landlord, at least thirty days before the termination date of the agreement or of any of its extensions, of her wish not to renew it. Failure to observe that period shall be penalised by the non-return of one month of the deposit.
The minimum legal period of performance of the lease is set at six months. Withdrawal by the tenant from the agreement before one year has elapsed, as agreed in this agreement, shall give rise to compensation equivalent to the proportional part of one month's rent in relation to the months remaining to complete one year.
In the event that this agreement is not extended, the tenant shall hand over the keys, and with them possession of the flat, to the landlord, vacated of persons.

FIVE. The parties may, thirty days before the end date of each extension, give notice of termination of the agreement by notifying the other party, reliably and in writing, of their withdrawal from it.

SIX. The dwelling covered by this agreement is made available to the tenant party on the first of July 2026, with floors, doors, windows, furniture, etc. in a good state of repair, and with the water and electricity installations working. The landlord is exempt from all liability for lack of the said supplies.
The tenant party undertakes to make proper use of the household appliances and, should they break down through use, the tenant party shall repair them or replace them with others of the same characteristics.

SEVEN. The property covered by this agreement shall be used solely as a dwelling, and consequently no offices for the exercise of liberal or other professions may be established in it; the tenant party further undertakes to keep the landlord informed if the smallest room is sublet, disclosing in writing the person who is going to live in the dwelling, with their identification details, latest payslip and employment contract. Likewise, keeping flammable materials, or materials harmful or dangerous to persons or to the leased flat, is prohibited. Likewise, installing signs and advertisements without the landlord's authorisation is prohibited. Keeping domestic animals must be notified in writing to the landlord, the tenant party being liable for any damage arising from the animal's use of the dwelling.

EIGHT. Carrying out works is prohibited unless they are authorised in writing by the landlord. Should the tenant party, with prior authorisation, carry out alterations and improvements, these shall revert to the landlord at the end of the agreement, without any compensation being claimable from her. All minor repairs, such as breakdowns, breakages or damage to the dwelling, that are shown to be due to improper use of the installations shall be borne by the tenant party.

NINE. The tenant party receives the dwelling covered by this agreement in habitable condition and undertakes to return it in the same condition on the day the agreement expires or, failing that, to pay in cash for the damage caused to the dwelling, the furniture or its services, authorising the landlord in the first place to recover it from the deposit lodged.

TEN. The tenant party shall pay the landlord the sum of NINE HUNDRED AND FIFTY EUROS (950 €), equivalent to one month's rent, to constitute the compulsory deposit established by law, which shall be returned on termination of the agreement once the tenant party has fulfilled, to the landlord's satisfaction, the stipulations arising from it, for which it stands as security. In no case may the amount of the deposit substitute for the monthly rent, so that any delay in paying the rent shall be considered a breach and grounds for eviction.

ELEVEN. The tenant party is responsible not only for its own acts but also for those committed by the occupants of the dwelling, as well as for any compensation for damages, and undertakes to report immediately and reliably to the landlord any incident affecting the property.

TWELVE. Breach of this agreement by the tenant party shall oblige it to pay all court costs, lawyer's and court agent's fees, the tax assessment of this agreement and any other costs that the landlord may incur to enforce it or to evict.

THIRTEEN. The tenant party undertakes to pay for the utilities, as well as the refuse collection charge, electricity, gas and internet. The homeowners' association fees (which include water) are borne by the landlord.

FOURTEEN. The parties submit, as required by law, to the jurisdiction of the Courts of the place where the leased dwelling is located, waiving the tenant party's own forum, if any.
